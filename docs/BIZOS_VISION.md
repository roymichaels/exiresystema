# BIZOS Vision Documentation

## Overview

BizOS is the **AI Business Operating System for all businesses**. It's a comprehensive platform that enables businesses to operate themselves through contextual AI advisors, business memory, and a complete suite of business management tools.

## Product Identity

### What BizOS IS
- **AI Business Operating System**: A platform that helps businesses run themselves through intelligent automation
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture**: Supports infinite businesses (tenants/workspaces) 
- **Business Primtes**: Reusable, configurable building blocks for business operations
- **Contextual AI Advisors**: AI that understands each business's unique context and needs

### What BizOS IS NOT
- **Not just a coaching CRM**: More than coaching, therapy, or counseling platforms
- **Not Shopify**: Not limited to e-commerce or product sales
- **Not Notion**: Not just note-taking or knowledge management
- **Not a generic CRM**: Comprehensive business operating system, not customer relationship management

## Core Business Operations

BizOS helps businesses manage through:

* **Contextual AI advisors** - Intelligent assistants that understand business context
* **Business memory** - Historical knowledge and learning
* **Leads** - Potential customer relationships
* **Customers/Clients** - Established business relationships
* **Tasks** - Work items and responsibilities
* **Follow-ups** - Ongoing communication and relationship maintenance
* **Payments** - Financial transactions and billing
* **Sessions/Appointments** - Scheduled interactions and meetings
* **Offers** - Products, services, and pricing
* **Products/Services** - Business offerings
* **Funnels** - Sales and conversion workflows
* **Content** - Marketing and educational materials
* **Campaigns** - Marketing initiatives
* **Workflows** - Business process automation
* **Automation** - Automated business operations
* **Insights** - Business intelligence and analytics
* **Reports** - Business performance documentation
* **Operations tracking** - Day-to-day business management
* **Growth intelligence** - Business growth analytics and recommendations

## Tenant Types

BizOS supports diverse business types:

* **Coaches, Therapists, Physiotherapists** - Professional services
* **Consultants** - Advisory services
* **Creators** - Content and media producers
* **Agencies** - Service-based businesses
* **Local Businesses** - Physical store operations
* **Online Businesses** - Web-based operations
* **Service Providers** - Various service offerings
* **Educators** - Teaching and training services
* **Communities** - Membership and engagement platforms

## Current Reality

**Exire Systema** is currently the first and only live tenant/workspace on BizOS. It represents Dean's own business implementation and serves as the foundation for the platform's development.

## Platform Architecture

BizOS operates through a clear separation of concerns:

1. **Platform Layer** - The core operating system infrastructure
2. **Tenant/Workspace Layer** - Individual business implementations
3. **Business Primitives** - Reusable business components
4. **AI Advisor Layer** - Contextual intelligence
5. **Workflow Engine** - Business process automation
6. **Automation Engine** - System operations
7. **Analytics Layer** - Business insights and reporting
8. **UI/Admin Shell Layer** - User interface and administration

## Business Owner Experience

BizOS provides business owners with:

- **Clear Visibility**: What needs attention now, what is leaking, what's next
- **Growth Block Identification**: Where business growth is being blocked
- **Contextual Intelligence**: AI that understands their specific business
- **Actionable Recommendations**: What to do to improve and grow
- **Memory Access**: Historical knowledge and learning
